Summary

Alba is an alarm clock. Your alarms, sounds, photos and preferences are stored only on your iPhone. Alba has no servers, doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't sell your data. The free version shows Google ads, which may use some data from your device, as explained below.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Alarms (time, days, name, challenge, sound and settings).
  • Sounds you import from Files or from a video.
  • Photos you choose for the challenge screen (a reduced copy is stored).
  • Objects you register for the scan challenge (name and code).
  • Preferences: theme, phrase, purchases and active passes.

Nobody else can access this data, not even us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Alarms: to schedule your alarms with iOS.
  • Camera: only to scan the code you chose for the challenge. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Motion & Fitness (optional): only to count your steps in the Walk challenge while the alarm rings. Nothing is saved or sent.
  • Notifications (optional): for "Are you still awake?" and to remind you before a free pass ends.
  • Photos: we use the iOS picker and only see the photos you choose.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Ads (free version)

Alba uses Google AdMob to show ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example, in the EU and UK).

  • No tracking: Alba doesn't ask for Apple's tracking permission, so ads don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or follow what you do in other apps.
  • Google may collect data such as a device identifier for this app, your IP address (to estimate an approximate location), device information, ad interactions and crash data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Ads are suitable for all ages.
  • You can change your consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Alba Pro shows no ads.

More information: Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and how Google uses data from partners' apps (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Children

Alba is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Since we don't store your data on servers, there's nothing for us to provide or delete: everything is on your iPhone and is removed with the app. For ad data, you can exercise your rights with Google and withdraw your consent from Settings. Questions: albalarmkit@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
